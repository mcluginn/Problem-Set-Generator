- **Course Code**: `GEN 0101`
- **Course Title**: `MATHEMATICS FOR ENGINEERS`
- **Source Document**: `GE_SYL_GEN0101_20260720 (2) - Copy.docx` (`SYL-005`)
- **Credits**: 3.0 units (Lecture)
- **Academic Term**: First Semester, Academic Year 2025–2026
- **Prerequisites**: None (`-`)
- **Official Course Description**:  
  > *"This course introduces essential mathematical concepts and techniques for engineering applications. Topics include basic algebra, polynomial operations, and both linear and nonlinear systems of equations. The course covers partial fraction decomposition, engineering trigonometry, and the solution of oblique triangles, emphasizing practical applications in engineering problems. Students will explore trigonometric identities and equations, exponential and logarithmic functions, as well as plane areas and volumes of solids with applications to engineering and science. The course also develops skills in rectangular coordinates, equations of lines, and conic sections, equipping students with analytical and problem-solving tools necessary for advanced engineering mathematics."*
