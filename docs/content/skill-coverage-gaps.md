## 2. Non-Calculus Course Expansion Queue (Planned for Future Phases)

The remaining five institutional courses will expand in future phases following the verified Calculus 1 pattern:
1. **`GEN 0101` (Mathematics for Engineers)**: Word problem modeling (Age, Mixture, Rate), right/oblique triangle trigonometry, and factoring.
2. **`GEN 0107` (Differential Equations)**: ODE classification, separable equations, integrating factor, and Laplace transforms.
3. **`GEN 0110` (Physics 2 for Engineers)**: Fluid statics/dynamics, heat conduction, DC circuit Kirchhoff loop equations, and optics.
4. **`GEN 0161` (Thermodynamics)**: First Law closed-system energy balances, ideal gas process work ($W = \int P dV$), steam table interpolation, and Rankine cycle efficiency.
5. **`BSIE 3219` (IE Special Topics 1)**: Compound interest, uniform gradient cash flows, depreciation schedules, and benefit-to-cost evaluations.
