## 2. Scale Gate Certification Criteria

Before unleashing automated mass generation on any course or problem family, the system enforces the **Five-Pillar Content Gate**:

1. **Pedagogical Evidence Definition**: Observable student action clearly defined in `ASSESSMENT_EVIDENCE_REGISTRY`.
2. **Deterministic Solution Verification**: Independent derivation or domain validator capable of verifying 100% of candidate solutions.
3. **5-Tier Hint Scaffolding**: Structured progressive assistance from concept reminder (L1) to full guided execution (L5) without premature answer leakage.
4. **Authentic Misconception Distractors**: Common student misunderstandings parameterized and linked to diagnostic error explanations.
5. **Teacher Calibration Benchmark**: A minimum batch of 10 problems per family reviewed across 6 quality dimensions with average rating $\ge 4.5/5.0$.
